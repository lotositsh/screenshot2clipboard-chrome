Template tokens
{YYYY} {YY} {MM} {DD} {hh} {mm} {ss} {ms} - date and time parts
{unix} - unix time in seconds, {unixms} - in milliseconds
{n} {n3} {n5} - counter (1, 001, 00001), grows with every upload
{rand} - 6 random chars, {rand12} - 12, {uuid} - random UUID
Any other text is kept as is. Extension is added automatically.
